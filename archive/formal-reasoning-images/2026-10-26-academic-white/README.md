# Academic monochrome figure restyle

The four current research figures were restyled in ChatGPT Create image through connected Chrome. Colored backgrounds, card fills, and decorative bands were replaced by white surfaces, dark outlines, and simple line symbols. Scientific content, source notes, layout, and website source links were retained.

The preceding published PNGs, WebPs, and ZIP were backed up, committed, and pushed before replacement. Source context:

- https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification
